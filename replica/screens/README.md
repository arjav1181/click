# Reference screenshots

Screenshots of the originals (Fireflies.ai, Granola, Otter.ai) collected from
public marketing pages, help centres and app-store listings live here.

Rules:

- Reference for layout comparison only. They are **never** shipped, bundled or
  imported by the app.
- They stay out of the published build and out of any marketing asset.
- Do not add screenshots from a logged-in account you do not own, from a
  paywalled area, or from private customer data.
- Prefer adding the source URL and date in the filename, e.g.
  `otter-conversation-page-2026-10-28.png`.

The directory is intentionally empty until the first capture pass.
